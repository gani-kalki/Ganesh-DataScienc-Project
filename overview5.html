SUMMARY / ANALYSIS

Time Series Analysis is used to work with data collected and organized according to time. In this experiment, Pandas provides tools for creating and manipulating timestamps, generating DatetimeIndex objects, handling different time zones and working with periods. The experiment also covers period arithmetic, frequency conversion and converting timestamp-based Series and DataFrame objects into periods. Finally, resampling techniques such as downsampling and upsampling are performed to change the frequency of time series data. These operations provide a foundation for understanding, processing and analyzing time-dependent datasets.
